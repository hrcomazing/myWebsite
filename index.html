What this site is really about

Good engineering is mostly making uncertainty smaller.

I like systems where the answer is not obvious at first. You have to read the old code, understand the interface, find the signal in the noise, and make the fix boring in the best possible way.

That thread runs through my avionics work, robotics project, manufacturing software, and AI tools: build a clear model, verify it, and leave the system easier to reason about than it was before.
